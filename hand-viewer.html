What do these stats mean?

Percentages use eligible hands or opportunities. Each cell shows its sample; hover for the definition. “—” means no opportunity. Clicking a seat or player opens their stats.

CB, Turn CB and River CB track continuation bets. W$SD and WWSF count any share of a split or side pot. bb/100 uses each hand’s big blind; it measures tournament chips, not ROI. Last stack is the latest recorded final stack.
